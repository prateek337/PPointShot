Training Programs

Comprehensive shooting courses designed for all skill levels - from beginners to Olympic aspirants

Basic Rifle Training

4-6 weeks

Fundamental rifle shooting techniques, safety protocols, stance, breathing, and basic marksmanship for beginners

Age Requirement: 12 years and above
Prerequisites: None - suitable for complete beginners
Price: ₹8,000 - ₹12,000
Includes: Equipment usage, ammunition, targets, safety gear, certificate

Advanced Rifle Training

6-8 weeks

Advanced techniques, precision shooting, competition preparation, and specialized training methods

Age Requirement: 15 years and above
Prerequisites: Completion of Basic Rifle Training or equivalent experience
Price: ₹15,000 - ₹20,000
Includes: Advanced equipment, competition targets, personal coaching

Basic Pistol Training

4-6 weeks

Pistol fundamentals, safety procedures, proper grip, stance, and accuracy training for beginners

Age Requirement: 18 years and above (as per regulations)
Prerequisites: None - suitable for beginners
Price: ₹10,000 - ₹15,000
Includes: Equipment usage, ammunition, targets, safety training

Advanced Pistol Training

6-8 weeks

Advanced pistol techniques, precision shooting, and competitive shooting preparation

Age Requirement: 18 years and above
Prerequisites: Completion of Basic Pistol Training or equivalent experience
Price: ₹18,000 - ₹25,000
Includes: Advanced pistol training, competition preparation

Competition Preparation

8-12 weeks

Intensive training for state, national, and international competitions including mental preparation

Age Requirement: As per competition categories
Prerequisites: Advanced level experience in rifle or pistol
Price: ₹25,000 - ₹35,000
Includes: Competition coaching, mental training, tournament support

Government Job Preparation

6-8 weeks

Specialized training for Army, Navy, Air Force, Police, CISF, Railways, CRPF recruitment

Age Requirement: As per recruitment requirements
Prerequisites: Basic shooting knowledge preferred
Price: ₹12,000 - ₹18,000
Includes: Job-specific training, mock tests, interview preparation

Private Sessions

Flexible

One-on-one personalized training sessions for rapid skill development

Age Requirement: 12 years and above
Prerequisites: All skill levels welcome
Price: ₹1,500 - ₹2,500 per session
Includes: Personal attention, customized training plan
